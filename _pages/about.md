I am an AI researcher at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL).

Before joining Meta, I earned my PhD from the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS)](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor [Yi Wu](https://jxwuyi.weebly.com/). I received my BEng from Tsinghua University's [Department of Electrical Engineering](https://www.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in 2021.

During my doctoral studies, I developed [AReaL](https://github.com/inclusionAI/AReaL), the first open-source asynchronous reinforcement learning training system for LLM agents. The project has received more than 5,500 stars on GitHub.

I approach research with the mindset of a programmer.

Outside of work, I enjoy coding, cooking, listening to pop music, and working out.
